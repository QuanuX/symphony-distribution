and no publisher profiles, credentials or hardware state. Finalize and inventory
the exact bridge before wrapping or running actual-client qualification. Verify
the package's source members against its selected client tree and preserve the
binary and catalog hashes across extraction.

identity and public contact links remain. Historical handoff ownership uses the
